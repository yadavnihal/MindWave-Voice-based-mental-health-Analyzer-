About MindWave

Mental Health Insights from the Way You Speak

MindWave is a research-grade voice emotion recognition platform that uses state-of-the-art self-supervised speech models to extract emotional features directly from raw audio waveforms.

Unlike keyword-based sentiment analysis, MindWave listens to how you speak — your tone, cadence, and vocal energy — to detect emotional states that text alone cannot reveal.

Features

Everything You Need to Understand Your Voice

Comprehensive mental wellness analytics, built on cutting-edge speech AI.

📊

Real-Time Segment Dashboard

Watch your emotional state evolve second-by-second. Interactive charts reveal emotion timelines, stress spikes, and audio energy patterns across your entire recording.

😰

Stress Score

Aggregated from negative emotion probabilities across all audio segments — gives you a single actionable number.

🔄

Mood Instability Index

Tracks how frequently your emotional state shifts throughout the recording, surfacing instability patterns.

🎵

Audio Energy Analysis

Measures vocal intensity and energy normalized across your recording — a proxy for emotional arousal.

💾

Session History

All your past analyses are saved locally for longitudinal tracking — revisit any session at any time.

🧠

7-Class Emotion Detection

Detects fear, anger, disgust, neutral, sadness, surprise and happiness with per-segment probability scores — not just a single label for the whole file.
